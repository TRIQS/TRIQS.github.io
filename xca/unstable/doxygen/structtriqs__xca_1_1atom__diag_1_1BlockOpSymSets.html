triqs_xca::atom_diag::BlockOpSymSets

#include <triqs_xca/atom_diag_utils.hpp>

Detailed Description

The field operators of an AtomDiag object, grouped into symmetry sets, without the barred operators.

The operators are grouped by identical c_connection row, i.e. by block-sparsity pattern, labeled in order of first appearance and stored in increasing orbital order within a set. This is the hybridization independent part of get_operators(), used by dynint::get_operators_and_interactions() to append the interaction symmetry sets before the quartet is built.
